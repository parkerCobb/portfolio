About Me:

I'm a business-minded designer focused on building inclusive experiences that work quietly and effectively for the people using them. I believe good design should remove friction, not add to it, and I use research and real user insight to create experiences that feel clear and intentional.

I approach design with close attention to accessibility, hierarchy, and flow. My background in marketing and IT shapes how I think about strategy and execution, helping me connect user needs to measurable business outcomes.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me doing various fiber arts, travelling to new places, watching procedural dramas, or dreaming about my future dogs.
